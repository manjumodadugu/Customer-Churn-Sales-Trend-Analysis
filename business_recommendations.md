# Business Recommendations

1. Prioritize retention activities for high-value and loyal customers.
2. Monitor at-risk customers whose recent purchase activity has declined.
3. Use monthly revenue trends to support inventory and promotional planning.
4. Review top-performing products and maintain suitable stock levels.
5. Compare customer segments using revenue, frequency, and recency rather than total sales alone.
6. Use country-level performance to identify markets for targeted campaigns and operational review.

These recommendations should be validated against current business data before implementation.
